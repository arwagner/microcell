## Label
A fold of the mitochondrion's inner membrane

## Paragraph
You are on a crista, one of the shelves the mitochondrion's inner membrane folds into [inv]. This membrane holds ATP synthase, the machine that makes ATP, the small molecule your cell spends as energy [mol]. The ATP synthases sit in bent pairs along the folds [proc].

## Go deeper
Other protein complexes in this membrane use energy from your food to pump protons (hydrogen ions) to one side of it, piling them up like water behind a dam. The protons flow back through ATP synthase, and that flow is what drives it [proc].
