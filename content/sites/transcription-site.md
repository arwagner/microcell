## Label
Where a gene is copied into RNA

## Paragraph
You are inside the nucleus, at a gene being read. Here RNA polymerase II, an enzyme (a protein that does one chemical job), copies the gene from DNA into messenger RNA, or mRNA: a working copy your cell can carry out of the nucleus [proc].

## Go deeper
Proteins called transcription factors gather at the start of the gene and call in the polymerase. It unwinds a short stretch of DNA into a "bubble", reads one strand, and builds a matching RNA chain one letter at a time, closing the DNA up behind it [proc].

Before the mRNA can be used, it gets a protective cap on its front end and a long tail on its back end, and stretches that don't code for protein are cut out. Then it leaves the nucleus through a nuclear pore, a gateway in the nucleus's wall [proc].
