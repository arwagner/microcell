## Label
Smooth ER, a web of fine tubes

## Paragraph
This is the smooth ER, the part of the ER network with no ribosomes on it. It is a web of fine tubes, each 30 to 60 nm across [inv]. It makes lipids, the fatty molecules that build membranes. It also stores calcium, which your cell releases in bursts as a signal to act [inv][ions].

## Go deeper
The smooth ER and the rough ER are one connected network, so your cell has one of it [used]. In a liver cell, the smooth ER holds 16% of the cell's membrane [mem], and the smooth ER and Golgi together fill 6% of the cell's volume [vol]. Its tubes touch other organelles to swap lipids with them [inv].
