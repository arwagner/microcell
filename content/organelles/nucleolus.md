## Label
The nucleolus, where ribosomes are built

## Paragraph
Inside the nucleus sits a dense blob called the nucleolus. It is the factory that builds ribosomes, the machines that make proteins [inv]. It can be a micrometer or more across [used]. Unlike most organelles, it has no membrane around it: it is a condensate, a blob that holds together without a wall [inv].

## Go deeper
A nucleus usually holds one to a few nucleoli [used]. Right after a human cell divides, each new nucleus starts with 10 small nucleoli, which then merge into fewer, larger ones [used]. That merging is why the app gives a range rather than one number.
