## Label
A mitochondrion, one of your cell's power plants

## Paragraph
This is a mitochondrion, one of your cell's power plants. It burns sugar and fat with oxygen to make ATP, the small molecule your cell spends as energy [inv]. It is about 1 micrometer (µm) long and has two membranes [used][inv]. The inner one folds into shelves called cristae, which hold the machines that make ATP [inv][mol].

## Go deeper
A HeLa cell, from a human cell line grown in labs, has roughly 350 to 770 mitochondria, more as it grows toward dividing [first][used]. A rat liver cell has about 1,665, and mitochondria fill 22% of a liver cell's volume, far more than in most cells [inv][vol].

In many living cells, mitochondria fuse and split, forming branching networks rather than separate beans [inv].
