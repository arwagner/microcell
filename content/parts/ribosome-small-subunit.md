## Label
The small subunit, which reads the message

## Paragraph
The small subunit holds the mRNA and checks that each codon is matched with the right tRNA, the adapter carrying the next amino acid [parts].

## Go deeper
To start, it binds the mRNA on its own and slides along it to the start codon, AUG, before the large subunit joins it [proc]. It is mostly long strands of RNA, with proteins bound to its surface [parts].
