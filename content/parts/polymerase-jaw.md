## Label
The jaw, which grips the DNA ahead

## Paragraph
Ahead of the copying point, the DNA still to be read rests against the jaw, part of one of the polymerase's 12 proteins [parts].

## Go deeper
The jaw belongs to the protein called RPB5. Its grip on the DNA seems to form already when copying first starts [parts].
