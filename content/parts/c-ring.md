## Label
The c-ring, the rotor in the membrane

## Paragraph
The c-ring sits in the membrane. Each proton that crosses hops onto one of its protein pieces, called c subunits, which lets the ring turn one notch; it hops off on the other side [proc][parts].

## Go deeper
Your c-ring has 8 c subunits, so 8 protons pass for each full turn: about 2.7 protons for every ATP made [proc][parts]. In the human structure the ring is about 5 nm across and 7 nm tall [size].
