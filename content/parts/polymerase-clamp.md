## Label
The clamp, which closes over the DNA

## Paragraph
The clamp is a movable part of the polymerase. In the yeast enzyme, the clamp stands open when the enzyme is free. Once it starts copying, the clamp swings shut over the DNA and the new RNA, holding them in place [parts].

## Go deeper
In the mammalian enzyme, the clamp shifts to fit the DNA and RNA as they bind, a fit that the strands themselves bring about [parts].
