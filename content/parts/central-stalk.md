## Label
The central stalk, the motor's axle

## Paragraph
The central stalk is fixed to the c-ring and turns with it, poking up into the head like an off-center axle. As it turns, it pushes each of the head's ATP-making sites into a new shape [proc][parts].

## Go deeper
It is mostly one protein, called gamma, with two partners, delta and epsilon [parts]. In the human structure it is about 12 nm long [size].
