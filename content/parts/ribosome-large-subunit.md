## Label
The large subunit, which links amino acids

## Paragraph
The large subunit holds the ribosome's active site, where each new amino acid is bonded onto the growing protein chain [parts].

## Go deeper
It joins the small subunit at the start codon, and the two split apart again when the protein is finished [proc]. Like the small subunit, it is mostly long strands of RNA with dozens of proteins on its surface [parts].
