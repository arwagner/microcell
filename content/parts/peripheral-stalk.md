## Label
The peripheral stalk, which holds the head still

## Paragraph
This arm runs up the side of the machine, from the membrane to the top of the head. It holds the head still so the axle can turn inside it [parts][proc].

## Go deeper
It is built from four proteins: OSCP, d, F6 and part of b [parts]. In the human structure it is about 21 nm long [size].
